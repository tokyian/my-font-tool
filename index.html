My font tool

by Arina Istomina, 2026

A playground where type designers can upload their own fonts and reshape them. If you're working with fonts that aren't your own, please check each font's license: make sure it allows changes and lets you use the modified version in your work.

If this tool helped you in your work with type, I'd love to see what you made — tag me on Instagram @tok228_. Thank you!

Questions? korumara@gmail.com

Made as part of my studies at the Royal Academy of Art, The Hague (KABK), for the Hacklab elective and the Coding class.
